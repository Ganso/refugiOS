¿Llegó íntegra la descarga? Comprueba la firma SHA256

Al ser ficheros grandes (~2.9 GB), una mala conexión puede corromper la descarga. Descarga SHA256SUMS.txt en la misma carpeta que el .zip y comprueba la integridad:

En Linux:

sha256sum --ignore-missing -c SHA256SUMS.txt

En macOS:

shasum -a 256 --ignore-missing -c SHA256SUMS.txt

En Windows (PowerShell):

Get-FileHash refugios-base-16G-es.img.zip -Algorithm SHA256

Debe devolver OK o coincidir exactamente con el hash del archivo de sumas.

Las instrucciones paso a paso, los requisitos y las guías completas están en GitHub.

Prepare it today at home with peace of mind

refugiOS is not a typical Linux distribution. It does not install on your hard drive or replace your operating system. It lives on a USB drive and boots on any PC or Raspberry Pi, working completely autonomously without installing anything on the host computer.

The concept is simple: prepare it today at home with a good Internet connection, so you have it ready the day there is no network. It carries a complete copy of Wikipedia, worldwide street mapping, an on-device local AI assistant, and encrypted personal vaults.

Knowledge

Full encyclopedia, technical survival guides, and medical manuals accessible without an Internet connection.

Mapping

Worldwide offline vector street maps and search capability without mobile network coverage.

Local AI

Integrated private artificial intelligence running entirely on your machine, requiring no remote cloud.

Encrypted Vaults

Strong encryption to protect personal records, critical credentials, and vital family documents.

Did the download arrive intact? Verify the SHA256 checksum

As these are large images (~2.9 GB), unstable connections may corrupt the file. Download SHA256SUMS.txt into the same folder and verify the checksum before flashing:

On Linux:

sha256sum --ignore-missing -c SHA256SUMS.txt

On macOS:

shasum -a 256 --ignore-missing -c SHA256SUMS.txt

On Windows (PowerShell):

Get-FileHash refugios-base-16G-en.img.zip -Algorithm SHA256

It must return OK or match the corresponding entry in the checksums file.
